About TechJobsBW

TechJobsBW is designed to bring job opportunities from across Botswana together in one convenient place.

We believe that finding a job should not require endless Google searches or visiting dozens of different company websites.

Our goal is to make the process easier by bringing opportunities from leading companies and organisations closer to you, all in one screen.

We also believe strongly in automation. Our systems regularly look for newly published opportunities so that jobs can be brought to you as soon as they become available.

TechJobsBW brings vacancies from organisations such as Mascom, First National Bank, Absa, Botswana Savings Bank, Water Utilities Corporation, Letshego, Statistics Botswana, and many many more.

Our mission is simple: make discovering career opportunities in Botswana easier, faster, and more convenient for applicants.
